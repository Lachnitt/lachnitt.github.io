\section{\textbf{PhD Research} \hfill \small{\textit{expected graduation: summer 2027}}}
\vspace{1mm}
\small{
My research has been centered around proof certificates for SMT solvers. My objective is to improve automation in the ITP Isabelle/HOL by reconstructing the SMT solver cvc5's proof certificates \ref{pub:itp25}.
